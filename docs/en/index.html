Frequently asked questions

Is MuscleCheck free?

Yes. The weekly checklist, streaks, history, statistics, widget, reminders, categories and the AI coach are free. MuscleCheck Pro (on iPhone) adds automatic workout detection with Apple Health and progress photos, for $1.99/month, $14.99/year or $29.99 lifetime.

Do I need an account?

No. No account, name or email needed: your data stays on your device.

Is it only for the gym?

No. It works for the gym, yoga, pilates, calisthenics, cardio, stretching or any activity, and you can create your own categories.

What does the AI coach do?

On iPhone with Apple Intelligence it suggests your training day: two muscle groups that go well together, with three example exercises each, based on what you've already trained. It only suggests; it never checks anything for you.

How is it different from apps like Strong or Hevy?

Those apps ask you to log every set, rep and weight. MuscleCheck tracks your week with one tap per muscle group or activity; logging weights or exercises is optional.

Does it work on Android?

Yes, on Android 8 or later: checklist, streaks, history, statistics, widget and reminders. The AI coach, Siri shortcuts, Apple Health integration and MuscleCheck Pro are iPhone-only.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your checklist and history live on your device, so logging a workout doesn't need the internet.

Which languages is it in?

English, Spanish, French and Italian.
